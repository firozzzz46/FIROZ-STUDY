PART 11

VERB & SENTENCE STRUCTURE

English Grammar & Bangla Grammar Equivalent Guide (Verb, Person, Number & Agreement)
লক্ষ্য: Verb কী, Verb-এর কাজ কী, Sentence-এ Verb কীভাবে শনাক্ত করতে হয়, Subject-এর সঙ্গে Verb-এর মিল, Main Verb ও Auxiliary Verb এবং Person & Number অনুযায়ী Verb-এর রূপ পরিবর্তন সম্পর্কে পরিষ্কার ধারণা তৈরি করা [cite: 47].

LESSON 1 - WHAT IS A VERB? (Verb / ক্রিয়া কী?)

Definition: A Verb is a word that shows an action, an event, or a state[cite: 47].

বাংলায়: Verb (ক্রিয়া) হলো এমন Word, যা কাজ, ঘটনা বা অবস্থা প্রকাশ করে[cite: 47].

Examples:
  • go (যাওয়া), eat (খাওয়া), read (পড়া), write (লেখা), sleep (ঘুমানো), run (দৌড়ানো)[cite: 47]
  • is/am/are (হওয়া/থাকা)[cite: 47]
সহজে মনে রাখো: ACTION + EVENT + STATE = VERB (Action = কাজ, Event = ঘটনা, State = অবস্থা)[cite: 47]

LESSON 2 - HOW TO IDENTIFY A VERB & BASIC SENTENCE STRUCTURE

Verb কীভাবে চিনবে?

বাক্যে Verb খুঁজে বের করার জন্য প্রথমে দেখো: ১. কে কী করছে? ২. কী ঘটছে? ৩. কার কী অবস্থা?[cite: 47]

Examples:

Rahim reads a book. (Rahim কী করছে? reads = Verb)[cite: 47]

The baby sleeps. (Baby কী করছে? sleeps = Verb)[cite: 47]

He is happy. (এখানে is Subject-এর অবস্থা বোঝাচ্ছে। is = Verb)[cite: 47]

SUBJECT + VERB & SUBJECT + VERB + OBJECT

English Sentence-এর সবচেয়ে গুরুত্বপূর্ণ ভিত্তি হলো Subject + Verb এবং এর সাথে Object থাকতেও পারে[cite: 47].

Examples:

Birds (Subject) fly (Verb)[cite: 47].

Rahim (Subject) reads (Verb) a book (Object)[cite: 47].

I (Subject) eat (Verb) rice (Object)[cite: 47].

Formula: S + V অথবা S + V + O[cite: 47]

LESSON 3 - SUBJECT + VERB + EXTENSION & MAIN VERB

SUBJECT + VERB + EXTENSION

Verb-এর সবসময় Object থাকবে না। সময়, স্থান, পদ্ধতি ইত্যাদির অতিরিক্ত তথ্য (Extension) থাকতে পারে[cite: 47].

Rahim works in Dhaka (Extension) | He runs quickly (Extension)[cite: 47].

MAIN VERB (মূল ক্রিয়া)

Main Verb হলো Sentence-এর প্রধান কাজ বা অবস্থা প্রকাশকারী Verb[cite: 47, 48].

I read books | She writes a letter | They play football | He sleeps[cite: 48].

সহজে: Main Verb = বাক্যের প্রধান কাজ/অবস্থা প্রকাশকারী Verb[cite: 48].

LESSON 4 - AUXILIARY VERB & AUXILIARY + MAIN VERB

Auxiliary Verb-কে Helping Verb-ও বলা হয়। এটি Main Verb-কে সাহায্য করে[cite: 48].

প্রধান Auxiliary Verbs[cite: 48]:
  • BE: am, is, are, was, were[cite: 48]
  • HAVE: have, has, had[cite: 48]
  • DO: do, does, did[cite: 48]
Formula: Auxiliary Verb + Main Verb[cite: 48]

He is (Auxiliary) reading (Main)[cite: 48].

They are (Auxiliary) playing (Main)[cite: 48].

She has (Auxiliary) finished (Main) the work[cite: 48].

They do (Auxiliary) not play (Main) football[cite: 48].

LESSON 5 - BE, HAVE & DO VERBS

1. BE VERBS (am, is, are, was, were)

এগুলো কখনো Main Verb হিসেবেও কাজ করতে পারে। যেমন: He is happy (He = Subject, is = Main Verb, happy = Adjective)[cite: 48]

2. HAVE VERBS (have, has, had)[cite: 48]
  • I have a book (have = Main Verb)[cite: 48]
  • I have finished my work (have = Auxiliary Verb)[cite: 48]
3. DO VERBS (do, does, did)[cite: 48]

এগুলো প্রশ্ন, Negative এবং জোর দেওয়ার কাজে Auxiliary Verb হিসেবে ব্যবহৃত হয় (যেমন: Do you play football?)[cite: 48].

গুরুত্বপূর্ণ: Do/Does/Did Auxiliary হিসেবে ব্যবহৃত হলে Main Verb সাধারণত Base Form-এ থাকে[cite: 48].

LESSON 6 - PERSON & NUMBER

PERSON[cite: 48]
  1. First Person (বক্তা নিজে): I, we[cite: 48]
  2. Second Person (যাকে বলা হচ্ছে): you[cite: 48]
  3. Third Person (যার সম্পর্কে বলা হচ্ছে): he, she, it, they, Rahim, Karim[cite: 48]
NUMBER[cite: 48]

Singular (একবচন যেমন: boy) ও Plural (বহুবচন যেমন: boys)[cite: 48]

বিশেষভাবে মনে রাখো: Third Person Singular হলো: He / She / It / কোনো নির্দিষ্ট একবচন নাম (যেমন: Rahim, Karim)[cite: 48]

LESSON 7 - SUBJECT-VERB AGREEMENT & S/ES RULES

Subject-এর সঙ্গে Verb-এর সঠিক মিল থাকতে হবে[cite: 49].

Present Simple Rule[cite: 49]:
  • I/You/We/They + Base Verb (সাধারণ Verb-এর সঙ্গে s/es যোগ হয় না। যেমন: I play. You play.)[cite: 49]
  • He/She/It + Verb-s/es (Third Person Singular Subject-এর সঙ্গে Verb-এ s/es যোগ হয়। যেমন: He plays.)[cite: 49]
S/ES RULES[cite: 49]
  1. সাধারণ নিয়মে S যোগ হয়: play → plays, read → reads[cite: 49]
  2. s, sh, ch, x, o থাকলে ES হয়: go → goes, watch → watches[cite: 49]
  3. Consonant + y হলে ies হয়: study → studies, try → tries[cite: 49]
  4. Vowel + y হলে শুধু s হয়: play → plays, enjoy → enjoys[cite: 49]

LESSON 8 - NEGATIVE SENTENCES & COMMON VERB MISTAKES

Present Simple Negative[cite: 49]

I do not play. | They do not play. | He does not play. | She does not play.[cite: 49]

খুব গুরুত্বপূর্ণ: does-এর পরে Main Verb-এর মূল রূপ (Base Form) হয়[cite: 49].

(যেমন: Incorrect ✗: He does not plays. | Correct ✓: He does not play.)[cite: 49]

Common Mistakes[cite: 49]

Incorrect ✗: He go to school. → Correct ✓: He goes to school.[cite: 49]

Incorrect ✗: They plays football. → Correct ✓: They play football.[cite: 49]

Incorrect ✗: She play football. → Correct ✓: She plays football.[cite: 49]

LESSON 9 - QUESTIONS & FULL SENTENCE ANALYSIS

QUESTIONS[cite: 49]

Present Simple Question-এ Do/Does ব্যবহৃত হয়। যেমন: Do you play football? বা Does he play football?[cite: 49]

খুব গুরুত্বপূর্ণ: Does-এর পরে Verb-এ s/es বসে না[cite: 49].

(যেমন: Incorrect ✗: Does he plays? | Correct ✓: Does he play?)[cite: 49]

FULL SENTENCE ANALYSIS[cite: 49]

Example: Rahim is reading a book.[cite: 49]

  • Rahim = Subject
  • is = Auxiliary Verb
  • reading = Main Verb
  • a book = Object[cite: 49]

Object সবসময় থাকবে কি? না। যেমন: Birds fly বা The baby sleeps বাক্যে কোনো Object নেই[cite: 49].

LESSON 10 - FINAL PRACTICE & MASTER SUMMARY

FINAL PRACTICE & EXERCISES[cite: 50]

A. Identify the Verb: Rahim reads a book. / The baby sleeps. / They play football. / She writes a letter. / He is happy. / We are studying English. [cite: 50]

B. Identify Main Verb and Auxiliary Verb: He is reading. / They are playing. / She has finished her work. / I have eaten rice. / He does not play football. / Did you go there? [cite: 50]

C. Choose the Correct Verb: He ___ to school. (go/goes) / They ___ football. (play/plays) / She ___ English. (study/studies) / I ___ books. (read/reads) / Rahim ___ TV. (watch/watches) [cite: 50]

D. Correct the Mistakes: He go to school. / She play football. / They plays cricket. / I goes home. / He does not plays. / Does she likes tea? [cite: 50]

E. Identify Person & Number: I, we, you, he, she, it, they, Rahim [cite: 50]
