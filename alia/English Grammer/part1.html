PART 1 — SENTENCE FOUNDATION

📚 English Grammar & Bangla Grammar Equivalent Guide

🎯 লক্ষ্য: Sentence (বাক্য) কী, Sentence-এর অংশ, Subject, Predicate, Object, Complement, Phrase, Clause, বাক্যের গঠন এবং বাক্যের প্রধান প্রকারভেদ সম্পর্কে মজবুত ভিত্তি তৈরি করা।
LESSON 1 — WHAT IS A SENTENCE? (Sentence কী?)

Definition: A Sentence is a group of words that makes complete sense.

🇧🇩 বাংলা: Sentence (বাক্য) হলো এমন কিছু শব্দের সমষ্টি, যা সম্পূর্ণ অর্থ প্রকাশ করে।

Example:
• Firoz reads a book. — ফিরোজ একটি বই পড়ে। (এখানে সম্পূর্ণ একটি অর্থ প্রকাশ পেয়েছে। তাই এটি একটি Sentence।)
• ❌ Firoz a book reads → শব্দগুলো আছে, কিন্তু সঠিকভাবে সাজানো নেই।
• ❌ Reads a book → সম্পূর্ণ অর্থ পরিষ্কার নয়।
• ✅ Firoz reads a book. → সম্পূর্ণ অর্থ প্রকাশ করছে।
LESSON 2 — MAIN PARTS OF A SENTENCE (বাক্যের প্রধান অংশ)

একটি সাধারণ Sentence-এর প্রধান দুটি অংশ:

  1. Subject → কর্তা
  2. Predicate → বিধেয়
Example: Fatiha plays football.
• Fatiha = Subject
• plays football = Predicate

📌 সহজে মনে রাখা: Subject = কে/কী? | Predicate = Subject সম্পর্কে কী বলা হচ্ছে?
LESSON 3 — SUBJECT & LESSON 4 — PREDICATE

Definition (Subject): The Subject is the person, animal, thing, or idea that the sentence is about.

🇧🇩 বাংলা: Subject হলো বাক্যে যার সম্পর্কে কিছু বলা হয় অথবা যে কাজটি করে।

Subject Examples:
• Firoz reads a book. (Who reads? → Firoz = Subject)
• The cat is sleeping. (Who is sleeping? → The cat = Subject)

Predicate: Predicate হলো Subject সম্পর্কে যা বলা হয়।

Predicate Examples:
• Firoz reads a book. (Subject: Firoz | Predicate: reads a book)
• The boy is playing football. (Subject: The boy | Predicate: is playing football)

📌 সূত্র: Subject + Predicate = Complete Sentence

LESSON 5 & 6 — BASIC SENTENCE PATTERNS

একটি সাধারণ English Sentence-এর সবচেয়ে গুরুত্বপূর্ণ ভিত্তি হলো Subject + Verb এবং Subject + Verb + Object।

Examples (Subject + Verb):
• Birds fly. — পাখিরা উড়ে। (Birds → Subject, fly → Verb)
• Firoz works. — ফিরোজ কাজ করে।
• Children play. — শিশুরা খেলে।
• The baby sleeps. — শিশুটি ঘুমায়।
Examples (Subject + Verb + Object):
• Fatiha reads a book. (Fatiha = Subject, reads = Verb, a book = Object)
• I eat rice. (I = Subject, eat = Verb, rice = Object)
LESSON 7 & 8 — OBJECT (DIRECT & INDIRECT)

Definition: Object হলো যার ওপর Verb-এর কাজটি প্রভাব ফেলে। সাধারণত Verb-এর পরে Object পাওয়া যায়।

• I read a book. (আমি কী পড়ি? → a book = Object)
• He bought a phone. (সে কী কিনল? → a phone = Object)

Direct & Indirect Object: কিছু বাক্যে দুটি Object থাকতে পারে।

Example: Firoz gave me a book.
• me → Indirect Object (কাকে দিল?)
• a book → Direct Object (কী দিল?)

Pattern: Subject + Verb + Indirect Object + Direct Object
• Fatiha gave her brother a pen. (her brother = Indirect Object, a pen = Direct Object)
LESSON 9 & 10 — COMPLEMENT

Definition: Complement হলো এমন একটি অংশ, যা Subject বা Object সম্পর্কে অতিরিক্ত প্রয়োজনীয় তথ্য দেয়।

• Firoz is a student. (a student = Subject Complement)
• He became a teacher. (a teacher = Complement)
• The sky is blue. (blue = Subject Complement)
• They made him captain. (captain = Object Complement)
• We found the book useful. (useful = Object Complement)
LESSON 11 TO 13 — WORD ORDER & SENTENCE PATTERNS

English-এ শব্দের অবস্থান খুব গুরুত্বপূর্ণ। সাধারণ Pattern: Subject + Verb + Object।

Important Basic Patterns:
1. S + V (Birds fly.)
2. S + V + O (I read books.)
3. S + V + C (Fatiha is a student.)
4. S + V + IO + DO (She gave me a pen.)
5. S + V + O + C (They made him captain.)
LESSON 14 — PHRASE & CLAUSE

Phrase: A Phrase is a group of words that does not contain a complete Subject-Verb structure and does not express a complete thought.

Clause: A Clause is a group of words that contains a Subject and a Verb.

Phrase vs Clause:
• Phrase: in the room (Subject + Verb নেই)
• Clause: when I came (I = Subject, came = Verb)

• Main Clause: I went home. (স্বাধীন)
• Subordinate Clause: because I was tired. (অধীন)
LESSON 15 — TYPES OF SENTENCE (BY STRUCTURE)
1. Simple Sentence: ১টি Independent Clause। (Ex: Firoz reads a book.)
2. Compound Sentence: ২টি Independent Clause + Conjunction। (Ex: I went to school, and I met Fatiha.)
3. Complex Sentence: ১টি Main Clause + ১ বা একাধিক Subordinate Clause। (Ex: I stayed home because it was raining.)
4. Compound-Complex: কমপক্ষে ২টি Independent + ১টি Subordinate Clause।
LESSON 16 — TYPES OF SENTENCE (BY PURPOSE)
• Assertive: He is honest. / He is not honest.
• Interrogative: Are you a student? (?)
• Imperative: Open the door. / Please help me.
• Exclamatory: What a beautiful flower! (!)
• Optative: May Allah bless you.
LESSON 17 — SUBJECT-VERB AGREEMENT & COMMON MISTAKES

Subject অনুযায়ী Verb-এর সঠিক রূপ ব্যবহার করা আবশ্যক।

❌ He go to school. ➔ ✅ He goes to school.
❌ I am go to school. ➔ ✅ I am going to school.
❌ She don't like tea. ➔ ✅ She doesn't like tea.
❌ Firoz a student is. ➔ ✅ Firoz is a student.
LESSON 18 & 19 — SENTENCE BUILDING & EXERCISES
Sentence Analysis Example:
Fatiha reads a good book every day.
• Fatiha (Subject) | reads (Verb) | a (Article) | good (Adjective) | book (Object) | every day (Adverbial)
Practice Corrections:
1. He go to school ➔ He goes to school.
2. She don't like tea ➔ She doesn't like tea.
3. Firoz a student is ➔ Firoz is a student.
