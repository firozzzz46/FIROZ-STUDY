LESSON 1 - CONJUNCTION: পরিচয়, কাজ ও প্রকারভেদ

Definition:

A Conjunction is a word that joins words, phrases, or clauses together [cite: 48].

Conjunction (সংযোজক অব্যয়) হলো এমন একটি শব্দ, যা দুটি বা ততোধিক Word, Phrase বা Clause-কে একসঙ্গে যুক্ত করে [cite: 48].

সহজে মনে রাখো: Conjunction = Joiner (অর্থাৎ যুক্তকারী শব্দ।) [cite: 48]
যেমন: and = এবং, but = কিন্তু, or = অথবা, so = তাই, because = কারণ, if = যদি, although = যদিও [cite: 48]

Examples:

  • Rahim and Karim: and দুটি Noun যুক্ত করেছে[cite: 48].
  • Tea or coffee: or দুটি Noun যুক্ত করেছে[cite: 48].
  • I came but he left: but দুটি Clause যুক্ত করেছে[cite: 48].

CONJUNCTION কী কী যুক্ত করে?[cite: 48]

  1. Word + Word (যেমন: Rahim and Karim are friends.)[cite: 48]
  2. Phrase + Phrase (যেমন: He likes reading and writing.)[cite: 48]
  3. Clause + Clause (যেমন: I went to school and I met Rahim.)[cite: 48]

KINDS OF CONJUNCTION (Conjunction-এর প্রকারভেদ)[cite: 48]

Conjunction-এর প্রধান ৩ প্রকার:[cite: 48]

  1. Coordinating Conjunction (সমন্বয়কারী সংযোজক)[cite: 48]
  2. Subordinating Conjunction (অধীনতাসূচক সংযোজক)[cite: 48]
  3. Correlative Conjunction (যুগ্ম সংযোজক)[cite: 48]

AND (and-এর ব্যবহার)[cite: 49]

and = এবং / ও। দুটি বা ততোধিক একই ধরনের শব্দ বা ধারণা যুক্ত করতে ব্যবহৃত হয় [cite: 49].

Examples: Rahim and Karim are friends. (রহিম ও করিম বন্ধু।) | I bought a pen and a book. | He reads and writes [cite: 49].

BUT (but-এর ব্যবহার)[cite: 49]

but = কিন্তু। বিপরীত বা contrast বোঝাতে ব্যবহৃত হয়। Pattern: A + but + B [cite: 49]

Examples: He is poor but honest. (সে গরিব কিন্তু সৎ।) | I tried but I failed [cite: 49].

OR (or-এর ব্যবহার)[cite: 49]

or = অথবা / নাকি। দুটি বা ততোধিক বিকল্প বোঝাতে ব্যবহৃত হয় [cite: 49].

Examples: Tea or coffee? | You can take tea or coffee [cite: 49].

SO (so-এর ব্যবহার)[cite: 49]

so = তাই / সুতরাং। ফলাফল বা consequence বোঝাতে ব্যবহৃত হয় [cite: 49].

Examples: It was raining, so I stayed home. | He was ill, so he could not come [cite: 49].

FOR, YET & NOR[cite: 49]

  • FOR: He stayed home, for he was ill. (এখানে for = Conjunction, আর "This gift is for you" এ Preposition)[cite: 49].
  • YET: He is poor, yet he is happy. (সে গরিব, তবুও সে সুখী - unexpected result)[cite: 49].
  • NOR: He does not smoke, nor does he drink. (ধূমপান করে না, মদ্যপানও করে না। Nor-এর পরে inversion হয়)[cite: 49].

LESSON 3 - SUBORDINATING CONJUNCTION: মূল ধারণা

যে Conjunction একটি Main Clause-এর সঙ্গে একটি Dependent/Subordinate Clause যুক্ত করে, তাকে Subordinating Conjunction বলে [cite: 49, 50].

প্রধান: because, if, although, though, when, while, before, after, since, unless, until, as, where, wherever, whether [cite: 49, 50]

Example Breakdown:[cite: 50]

Sentence: I stayed home because it was raining.[cite: 50]

- Main Clause: I stayed home[cite: 50].

- Subordinate Clause: because it was raining[cite: 50].

BECAUSE & IF[cite: 50]

  • BECAUSE (because + Clause): I stayed home because it was raining. (বৃষ্টি হচ্ছিল বলে বাড়িতে ছিলাম।)[cite: 50]
  • IF (If + Condition, Result): If you work hard, you will succeed. (তুমি কঠোর পরিশ্রম করলে সফল হবে।)[cite: 50]

UNLESS & ALTHOUGH / THOUGH[cite: 50]

  • UNLESS (unless = if...not): You will fail unless you study. (পড়াশোনা না করলে ব্যর্থ হবে।)[cite: 50]
  • ALTHOUGH / THOUGH: Although he is poor, he is honest. / Though it was raining, we went outside[cite: 50].

LESSON 4 - TIME, REASON, CONDITION & UNCERTAINTY

WHEN/WHILE & BEFORE/AFTER[cite: 50]

  • When / While: When I came, he was sleeping. | While I was reading, he came (while চলমান কাজের সময় বসে)[cite: 50].
  • Before / After: Wash your hands before you eat. | I went home after I finished the work[cite: 50].

SINCE / AS, UNTIL & WHETHER[cite: 50]

  • Since / As: Since you are here, help me. | As it was raining, we stayed home (যেহেতু / কারণ)[cite: 50].
  • Until: Wait until I come. / Stay here until evening (যতক্ষণ না / পর্যন্ত)[cite: 50].
  • Whether: I don't know whether he will come. / Whether you agree or not, I will go (কিনা / কি না / অনিশ্চয়তা)[cite: 50].

LESSON 5 - CORRELATIVE CONJUNCTION (যুগ্ম সংযোজক)

যে Conjunction জোড়ায় জোড়ায় ব্যবহৃত হয়, তাকে Correlative Conjunction বলে [cite: 50].

Pair Meaning & Example
both ... and উভয়ই...এবং (Both Rahim and Karim are students.)[cite: 50]
either ... or হয়...নয়তো (Either Rahim or Karim will come.)[cite: 50]
neither ... nor না...না (Neither Rahim nor Karim came.)[cite: 50]
not only ... but also শুধু...নয়, বরং...ও (He is not only intelligent but also honest.)[cite: 50]

LESSON 6 - CORRELATIVE CONJUNCTION: AS...AS, SO...THAT & SUCH...THAT

  • AS ... AS (Equality / সমতা): Rahim is as tall as Karim. (রহিম করিমের মতোই লম্বা।)[cite: 51]
  • SO ... THAT (so + Adjective/Adverb + that + Clause): He was so tired that he could not walk. (সে এত ক্লান্ত ছিল যে হাঁটতে পারেনি।)[cite: 51]
  • SUCH ... THAT (such + a/an + Adj + Noun + that): It was such a beautiful day that we went outside. (দিনটি এত সুন্দর ছিল যে বাইরে গেলাম।)[cite: 51]

LESSON 7 - CONJUNCTION vs PREPOSITION, ADVERB & CLAUSE

Conjunction vs Preposition[cite: 51]

একই শব্দ কখনো Preposition, আবার কখনো Conjunction হতে পারে [cite: 51].

• Before lunch, I prayed. (before + Noun = Preposition)[cite: 51]

• Before I ate lunch, I prayed. (before + Clause = Conjunction)[cite: 51]

Conjunction vs Adverb[cite: 51]

• He was tired, so he slept. (so = Conjunction)[cite: 51]

• He was tired; therefore, he slept. (therefore = Adverb)[cite: 51]

Clause + Conjunction[cite: 51]

• I studied hard because I had an exam. (because দুটি Clause যুক্ত করেছে) [cite: 51].

LESSON 8 - PUNCTUATION, PARALLEL STRUCTURE & COMMON MISTAKES

Punctuation with Conjunction[cite: 51]

দুটি সম্পূর্ণ Independent Clause, Coordinating Conjunction দিয়ে যুক্ত হলে অনেক ক্ষেত্রে Conjunction-এর আগে Comma (,) বসে। যেমন: I was tired, but I continued working [cite: 51].

Parallel Structure[cite: 51]

Conjunction দিয়ে যুক্ত অংশগুলোর গঠন একই ধরনের হতে হয় [cite: 51].

• Incorrect ✗: He likes reading and to write[cite: 51].

• Correct ✓: He likes reading and writing[cite: 51].

Common Mistakes (সাধারণ ভুল ও সংশোধন)[cite: 51, 52]

Incorrect (ভুল ✗) Correct (সঠিক ✓)
Although he is poor but he is honest.[cite: 51] Although he is poor, he is honest.[cite: 51]
Because it was raining, so I stayed home.[cite: 51] It was raining, so I stayed home.[cite: 51]
Unless you do not study, you will fail.[cite: 51] Unless you study, you will fail.[cite: 51]
Both Rahim or Karim came.[cite: 51] Both Rahim and Karim came.[cite: 51]

LESSON 10 - FINAL PRACTICE & KEY TAKEAWAY

A. Identify the Conjunction:[cite: 52]

  1. Rahim and Karim are friends[cite: 52].
  2. I tried but I failed[cite: 52].
  3. Tea or coffee?[cite: 52]
  4. I stayed home because it was raining[cite: 52].
  5. If you work hard, you will succeed[cite: 52].

B. Fill in the blanks:[cite: 52]

  1. Rahim ___ Karim are friends. (and)[cite: 52]
  2. He is poor ___ honest. (but)[cite: 52]
  3. Hurry up ___ you will miss the bus. (or)[cite: 52]
  4. I stayed home ___ I was sick. (because / as)[cite: 52]
  5. ___ you study, you will pass. (If)[cite: 52]

C. Correct the mistakes:[cite: 52]

  1. Although he is poor but he is honest. → Although he is poor, he is honest [cite: 52].
  2. Because he was sick, so he stayed home. → Because he was sick, he stayed home [cite: 52].
  3. Unless you do not study, you will fail. → Unless you study, you will fail [cite: 52].

CONJUNCTION - KEY TAKEAWAY (মূল কথা)

Conjunction হলো এমন একটি শব্দ যা Word, Phrase বা Clause-কে যুক্ত করে [cite: 52].

AND = যোগ, BUT = বিপরীত, OR = বিকল্প, SO = ফলাফল, BECAUSE = কারণ, IF = শর্ত, ALTHOUGH = যদিও [cite: 52].
